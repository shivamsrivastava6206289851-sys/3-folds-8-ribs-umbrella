# 3-Fold, 8-Rib Umbrella Mechanism

Designed and developed a 3D CAD model of a **3-fold umbrella mechanism with 8 ribs** using SolidWorks. The project focuses on the mechanical design of a foldable structure that enables compact storage and efficient deployment.

### Key Features
- **3-Fold Mechanism:** Enables the umbrella structure to fold into a compact form.
- **8-Rib Framework:** Provides structural support for the canopy and helps distribute loads.
- **CAD Modeling:** Developed 3D models of the mechanical components and assembled them to represent the complete mechanism.
- **Assembly and Motion:** Focused on the arrangement and movement of ribs and folding links during opening and closing.

### Tools Used
- SolidWorks — 3D Part Modeling and Assembly Design

### Project Objective
To understand and implement the kinematics of a foldable mechanism through CAD modeling, component assembly, and visualization of its opening and closing motion.
